# Photo-derived cutouts

Created with built-in imagegen editing from the reusable photographs in `../photographs/`. These are AI-isolated and enlarged interpretations, not pixel-exact crops or scientific measurements. The original source photographs, credits, license links and scale bars remain in the ParticleAtlas detail panels. CSS adds a cool low-light treatment, gentle drift and interaction highlights; relative sizes and movement are illustrative.

`fiber.webp`: derived from panel C of Richard L. Reynolds / USGS, JGR Atmospheres fiber image, CC BY 4.0. `pellet.webp`: derived from Gabriele85's CC0 pellet photograph. `fragment.webp` and the nine sheet crops: derived from Andrew Spanjer / USGS public-domain fragment photograph.

The nine independent sprites are cream, ring, green, turquoise, clear, foam, black, grey, and chip. The green single-subject edit is also retained as fragment.webp. Rectangular slicing and WebP encoding preserve the alpha produced by imagegen. No stock thumbnails were incorporated. The user's Shutterstock links inform only the general underwater density and cool lighting direction; reuse licenses for those exact images were not confirmed.
